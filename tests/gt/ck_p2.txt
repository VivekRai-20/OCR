Instantaneous Rate
The rate of reaction at a given instant of time is called as instantaneous rate of reaction.
To determine instantaneous rate of reaction, a graph of concentration versus time is plotted, a curve is obtained and the tangent is drawn at a point on a curve where the slope is rate of reaction.
[R] slope = -d[R] / dt Time
[P] slope = d[P] / dt Time
Instantaneous rate of consumption of R = -d[R] / dt
Instantaneous rate of formation of P = d[P] / dt
Instantaneous rate of Reaction = -d[R] / dt = d[P] / dt
Unit = Ms-1 = mol dm-3 s-1
