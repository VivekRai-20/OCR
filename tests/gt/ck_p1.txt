CHEMICAL KINETICS
Chemical Kinetics is branch of chemistry that deals with the rates of reaction or factors affecting rate and the mechanism of the reaction.
RATE OF REACTION
The rate of chemical reaction is defined as the change in concentration of a designated species i.e. reactant or product in the given time interval.
AVERAGE RATE
It is defined as change in concentration of reactant or product in a given interval of time.
Consider a reaction
R -> P
Avg. rate of Rxn = Δ[c] / Δt
Because rate cannot be negative
Avg. rate of consumption of R = -Δ[R] / Δt
Avg. rate of formation of P = Δ[P] / Δt
Δ[c] / Δt = -Δ[R] / Δt = ΔP / Δt
Unit = M/s = mol/dm3 s
