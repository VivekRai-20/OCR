ORDER OF REACTION
Consider a general reaction
aA + bB -> cC + dD
If the rate of reaction is experimentally determined
Rate = K [A]^x [B]^y
Then the sum of x+y gives overall order of reaction. Thus, overall order of reaction is given as the sum of power of the concentration terms in the rate law expression.
Example
2H2O2 -> 2H2O + O2
Rate = K [H2O2]^1 1st order
NO2 + CO -> CO2 + NO
Rate = k [NO2]^2 [CO]^0
2nd order w.r.t NO2 Zero w.r.t CO
CH3CHO -> CH4 + CO
Rate = [CH3CHO]^3/2 Experimentally determined value
H2 + I2 -> 2HI
Rate = K [H2]^1 [I2]^1
